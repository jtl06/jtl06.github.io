libdivide ARM SVE/SVE2

Scalable vector division for signed and unsigned 16/32/64-bit integers, with SVE and SVE2 backends contributed upstream.

Performance across backends

On this Graviton 4 workload, the SVE and SVE2 backends deliver about 2.2× and 2.3× NEON throughput across the 32/64-bit types.

Measurement details

Times are arithmetic means of matched per-divisor medians, including branchfull and branchfree algorithms. Multipliers divide the mean NEON time by the mean SVE or SVE2 time before rounding. The overall summary geometrically averages those multipliers across the four 32/64-bit types. Nine samples per case; automatic vectorization disabled. Timings measure array-loop throughput per element, not individual instruction latency. SVE and SVE2 refer to the selected library backends, both including the predicated-negation optimization and compiled for the native CPU; the SVE column is not an SVE-only instruction-set limit. NEON was restricted to Armv8-A. These are fixed-divisor array-loop measurements; individual cases can favor NEON.

Implementation

SVE provides native vector operations for 16-bit division and vector multiply-high instructions for 64-bit division. The SVE2 backend adds halving-add instructions for unsigned 16/32-bit corrections; signed paths use predicated bias and sign adjustments. The work includes both division algorithms, tests, CMake integration, and documentation.
